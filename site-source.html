Design culture spotlight

Virgil Abloh: remix with intention

Abloh's widely discussed “3% rule” is often summarized as changing a small part of an existing idea to make it feel new. Treat that as a prompt for critical iteration—not permission to copy. His Off-White visual language made quotation marks, industrial labels, and familiar graphic conventions feel deliberately self-aware.

Interpretive summary; not a direct quotation. Study context, credit sources, and make meaningful original work.
